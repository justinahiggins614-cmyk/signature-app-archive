📱 How to use The Signature App Archive

Every app here is an original Signature-line program. Here is what everything on this site does, in plain words.

🔎 Search

Type an app name, feature words, or an exact app ID like JAH-APP-000001. The category menu and the A–Z buttons narrow the results. If nothing matches, the page says so plainly — try different words.

🧭 Finder AI

Describe the app you need in plain words — for example, something to track my shop inventory — and tap Find it. It matches archived apps by meaning and shows why each one matched. It never invents apps.

🗂️ App categories

All 30 app categories, one card each — word processors, spreadsheets, email, games, and more. Tap a card to see that category's newest apps, then ← All apps to come back.

✨ Fresh from the archive

The newest apps added. Every card opens the app's full record page.

📄 The app record page

  • About — what the app is, its provenance, and that it runs offline.
  • App status — a plain table: the browser demo is a DEMO of the app class; the downloads are the complete programs.
  • Features, How to use, System requirements, Version history — the full file.

▶️ Live demo

The demo runs the app class's core function right in your browser, on your device — no network calls. It demonstrates the class; the downloads are the full programs.

⬇️ Downloads

  • Copy — copies the full app text.
  • .txt / .json — the app file as text or data.
  • Single-file app (.html) — the whole app in one file; works offline, double-click to run.
  • Python source (.py) — the app's core function as a Python program.
  • Thumbdrive / CD-ROM pack (.zip) — everything above plus a manifest (each file's CRC32 check code). Copy it to a thumbdrive or burn it to a disc; nothing is uploaded, it is built in your browser.

🔊 Read aloud

Reads the whole app record. The bottom bar shows your place; Pause/Resume and Stop control it.

🤖 Ask the AI helper

Answers only from the app's own file — features, how to use it, requirements, versions, downloads. It also says hello and handles small talk like a person.

✅ Verify · ⚠ Report · ↗ Share/Open

Verify re-solves the app from its seed to prove the record is deterministic. Report problem copies a problem report you can send to the archive keeper. Share copies the app's link; Open opens it in a new tab.

🔗 Machine links

At the bottom of the home page: api.json, the app schema, an example record, archive health, the sitemap, and the architecture notes.
